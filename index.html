About

Engineering leadership, forged in the Motor City

I'm a Metro Detroit native and software engineering leader with 15+ years of experience spanning public safety systems and connected vehicle software. I got my start modernizing mission-critical software for police, fire, and corrections agencies before moving into the automotive world. Over the last decade-plus, I've built everything from early web platforms to modern Android platforms, delivering enterprise-scale infotainment apps for Ford and General Motors that are now installed in over 13 million vehicles across North America.

I've led every stage of the software lifecycle: architecture, hands-on engineering, product strategy, and people leadership. Whether coaching engineers, shipping first-of-its-kind manufacturing tools, or driving cross-functional roadmaps with product, UX, and cybersecurity teams, I care about building durable systems and durable teams.
